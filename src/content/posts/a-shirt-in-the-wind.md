The rain stops.\
On the washing line\
a yellow shirt\
fills with the wind.

Two wooden pegs\
hold its shoulders.\
Its sleeves lift,\
then settle.

Another gust,\
and both arms open.

From the kitchen window\
it looks, for a moment,\
like someone\
glad to see me.
