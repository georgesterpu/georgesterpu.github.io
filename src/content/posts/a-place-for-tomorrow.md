At bedtime, Nora found a feather\
in the pocket of her coat.\
She meant to show her brother.

She set it in a little red bowl\
beside his waiting boots.

By morning there were spoons to find,\
a sleeve turned inside out,\
rain at the window.

The feather waited.

Her brother stopped.\
“Where did this come from?”

The spoons could wait a moment.

They held it to the window.\
A hundred little branches\
caught the morning light.

“Shall we look for the bird?”\
he asked.

Nora reached for her coat.
